## Village prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original game-ready environment background for Small Hero, a cozy but adventurous 2D isometric action RPG. Landscape 1536 by 1024. Full-bleed detailed hand-painted pixel-art environment, crisp richly textured pixel clusters, classic late-1990s fantasy RPG aesthetic. Elevated three-quarter orthographic view with no horizon. A small medieval blacksmith hamlet bordering an emerald forest. At upper left a mossy ruined stone tower and a timber smithy with a muted sand-colored canvas awning, warm orange forge, tools and barrels. At lower left a teal river and weathered wooden footbridge. Broad open walkable ochre earth and short grass clearing occupies center and center-right, branching paths going to upper right and lower right into forest. Dense beautiful oak canopies, ferns, tiny flowers and scattered rocks frame the edges. Sunlit warm afternoon, deep forest-green shadows, earthy browns, moss, gold, teal water. Small environment details; world should feel large enough for tiny adventurers. Path network and empty central clearing must be clear and navigable. Absolutely no people, characters, monsters, UI, labels, letters, text, borders or logos. This is a usable environment texture under a separate live game layer, not a screenshot or UI mockup.

## Rendering

Cinderwing uses an original transparent four-pose sprite atlas, generated with the built-in tool. Source prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent pixel-art sprite sheet for an isometric medieval browser RPG called Small Hero. Generate ONE square PNG sprite sheet with real transparent alpha background, exactly 2 columns by 2 rows, equal square cells, no gutters, no grid lines. In each cell show the SAME small dragon miniboss, Cinderwing, full body with wings and curled tail entirely visible and centered with generous transparent padding. Consistent size, feet baseline, perspective and position between all four frames. Dragon occupies about 76 percent of each cell width. Style: richly detailed hand-painted pixel art, crisp pixel edges, warm burnt red scales, copper orange wing membranes, charcoal horns, little glowing amber eyes, cute but formidable young dragon, 3/4 overhead isometric camera about 45 degrees down, dragon faces lower left. Matches mossy medieval stone village RPG art. Top-left frame: idle, wings partly folded, mouth closed. Top-right frame: inhaling, neck arched, chest swelling, orange mouth glow, wings lifting. Bottom-left frame: breathing pose, mouth wide open facing lower left, wings extended, bright mouth glow, NO external fire plume (fire will be drawn in the engine). Bottom-right frame: recovering, head lowered slightly, wings settling. No text, labels, borders, scenery, floor, checkerboard pattern or cast shadow; transparent background throughout. No extra dragons or body parts. Produce a square 1024x1024 image with four equally sized 512x512 cells.

The output's actual dimensions are inspected before configuring frame sizes. Fire and its warning cone are drawn by Phaser from the server's attack coordinates; they are not baked into the sprite.

The village texture stays static; Phaser renders live actors, walking, slime motion, target rings, health bars, damage numbers, forge/camp effects, and motes above it. Inventory art uses matching atlas frames. The early hero uses a single directional sprite with reflection and motion cues; a full directional walk/attack animation set is a future art task.

Local font packages: Cinzel and Nunito Sans. Interface icons: Lucide. Their upstream licenses are included in the installed packages.
